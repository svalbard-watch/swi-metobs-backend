# Terms of Service

> **Placeholder.** This instance of the software has no terms of service configured.

The operator of this instance must publish their own terms of service. To do so, set the
`SWI_LEGAL_REPO` environment variable of the metobs backend to a GitHub repository (`owner/repo`)
that publishes releases tagged `terms-of-service-vX.Y.Z` containing `terms_of_service_EN.md`.

Until then, the service is provided without any terms stated here.
